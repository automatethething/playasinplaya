Check that your phone is unlocked and eSIM-capable before buying a plan. Compare coverage where you will stay, data limits, hotspot rules, renewal dates, and support channels—not only the headline price.

Install the eSIM while you still have reliable Wi-Fi and save the provider’s support instructions. Keep a backup connection for travel day and important work calls.
