For an emergency, call 911 in Mexico or ask a nearby trusted person, hotel, or medical provider to help contact emergency services. If it is safe, bring identification, insurance information, a medication list, and a way to contact someone you trust.

This page cannot assess symptoms or recommend a provider. Seek qualified medical help promptly for severe, sudden, or worsening symptoms.
