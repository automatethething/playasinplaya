Take a day pass before buying a long membership. Test the Wi-Fi where you would actually sit, check the call policy, and ask about backup power, meeting-room access, and taxes or deposits.

Hours, pricing, and noise levels change. Confirm the current details directly with the space, especially during high season or before an important call.
