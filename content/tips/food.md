Start with places that are busy, clean, and clear about prices. Ask about ingredients if you have allergies, and confirm whether service or tax is already included before paying.

Food tolerance is personal. Make conservative choices when you first arrive, carry any prescribed medication, and seek professional care for serious or persistent symptoms.
