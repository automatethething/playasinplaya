For a taxi, agree on the fare before the ride and keep enough cash for the trip. For walking, choose well-lit routes that you are comfortable with and adjust plans for weather and time of day.

For regional transport, confirm the departure point, destination, fare, and last return option on the day. Timetables and pick-up locations are not guaranteed.
