Search nearby, then check recent opening-hour updates and reviews rather than assuming an older recommendation is current. Busy morning spots can be loud or have limited tables.

If you need to work, ask before ordering whether laptops and power outlets are welcome. A café is a business first: buy regularly, use headphones, and leave space for other guests.
